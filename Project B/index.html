Project B: Emotion Lottery Machine

What will the world be like if people all have synethesia thousands years later?
Instead of being described with language, each feeling is expressed through movement, sound, and color—like a synesthetic creature whose identity cannot be fully captured by words. This machine becomes a metaphor for our emotional life: a mechanism that produces feelings at random, each arrival unpredictable and fleeting.

HOW TO PLAY???
Before entering the lottery, you can hover on each object to preview each "emotion". Feel this emotion based on the motion and the sound. To draw an emotion from this lottery machine, please push the button on the right. You can't choose which one you get, and sometimes feelings show up when you don't want them—just like real life.
